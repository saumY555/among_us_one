How a round works

You don’t need to know the video game. We explain everything at the start, and every round takes about fifteen minutes.

Do your tasks

Crewmates collect tokens hidden around the hall to fill the ship’s progress bar. Fill it and the crew wins.

Spot the impostor

Two players per round secretly sabotage the ship. Watch who disappears, who lies, and who acts too calm.

Hold the meeting

Found something suspicious? Call a meeting, argue your case, and vote. The person with the most votes is ejected.

The plan for the night

Arrive by 2 PM so we can sort you into crews before round one.
